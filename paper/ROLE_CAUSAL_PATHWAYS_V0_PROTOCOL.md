## Joint intervention

Query index encodes event bit 2 and role bit 1. For each recipient query q, target q XOR mask, mask in {0,1,2,3}. Test candidate pair and control pair, both fixed-clean-donor and dynamic variants. Fixed: replace projected readout head output with corresponding clean donor head output from same source and matched query; for cross-layer pairs the later head target is fixed even if the earlier intervention alters its native output. Dynamic: at each intervened layer compute current head outputs after all preceding layer interventions, then swap corresponding outputs across the simultaneously intervened query batch. At a shared layer both head swaps act on the same pre-intervention activation state. For cross-layer pairs the physical forward order is fixed; no fictitious reverse execution order. Dynamic and fixed agree for single-head interventions up to floating point tolerance; simultaneous interventions may differ. Dynamic donor at later layer is itself affected by earlier patch, hence it is not a clean donor. Neither tests complete circuit sufficiency. Use target candidate accuracy, all-four target correctness, full-vocabulary target probability, event mass and conditional role probability for the target event; also original query metrics and baseline-relative deltas. For event swaps use the role conditional on the donor event, not the original-event conditional role. No claiming independent composition solely from separate single swaps. A strong descriptive signature is high joint target accuracy and both target factor probabilities on both populations and both seeds, with much weaker controls; compare against intact and single swaps. No post-hoc threshold is confirmatory.

## Hybrid Q/K/V tests

At each selected head (role and event candidates, both crossed query axes, and two matched controls on their respective axes), reconstruct the projected contribution at the readout from Q_readout, K_all, V_all and W_O slice. Swap clean donor Q, K, V, QK, QV, KV or QKV; also swap attention pattern alone (recipient V) and pattern+V. For K/V use additional swaps restricted to the single changed input-token position (K only, V only, KV) and KV at all other positions. Recipient causal mask and W_O remain fixed. QKV and pattern+V must reconstruct clean donor projected head output within numerical tolerance. These tests isolate activation-level dependencies under hybrid counterfactuals; they do not directly intervene on learned W_O parameters, establish natural mediation, or prove that token-position patching captures all upstream paths. K/V at later layers may contain contextualised information from other positions. Pattern-only swaps may be off-manifold. Compare target accuracy and probabilities to clean baseline and full head swap. No cross-model activation swaps.

## Controlled downstream restoration

After swapping the candidate role or event head, individually restore clean recipient readout output of each later head or MLP (including same-block MLP). Recompute all subsequent layers normally. A reduction in donor effect indicates that downstream component matters under this controlled intervention; it is not direct edge attribution or proof of necessity under all contexts. No restoration of earlier components or same-layer parallel heads. Report all tested destinations, not just best ones. Full position-level tracing, entity-conditioned retrieval, natural-versus-induced activation geometry, minimal circuit sufficiency/pruning and temporal crosscoding are deferred to separate stages.
