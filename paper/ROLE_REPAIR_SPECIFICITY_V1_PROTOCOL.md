# ROLE-REPAIR-SPECIFICITY-v1 — Stage A

Prospective follow-up conditional on the selected seed 27114 checkpoint at 51,200 updates. Not independent initialisation replication. Eight independent deterministic resampled continuation schedules, paired across eight arms. Schedules restart sampling counters and use the original eligible training assignments and surface cells, not held-out examples. Fresh AdamW for all arms, original LR and weight decay, CE or event NLL + 4 role NLL. Target L0.head0, L0.mlp, L2.mlp; random controls use distinct head/MLP sets with minimum overlap and matched scalar count; layer controls retain both MLPs and replace the layer-0 head, with 3/3/2 balance. Random sets vary with schedule; paired contrast is across these predefined combinations, not a crossed estimate of schedule and mask effects. No outcome-based selection.

Primary: mean paired target_weighted minus layer_weighted restricted strict four-query correctness at 1600 updates, equally averaging both held-out populations and voices. Secondary contrasts as in manifest, final performance, first observed checkpoint above or equal to 95%, event and role metrics, final single-head causal diagnostics. Exact sign-flip calculation descriptive under exchangeability; not a general population inference. No multiplicity adjustment for exploratory secondary comparisons. All arms run 6400 updates, no early stopping. Same checkpoint and evaluation benchmark as previous study; independent schedules only. Parameter matching is not compute matching. Stage B subsets and Stage C resistant models are not implemented in this package and require separate protocols. Do not modify previous studies.
