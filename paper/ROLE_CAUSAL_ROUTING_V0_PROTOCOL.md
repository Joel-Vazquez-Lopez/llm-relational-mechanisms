# ROLE-CAUSAL-ROUTING-v0

## Status and question
Exploratory discovery on already inspected recovery seeds. No new Transformer training. Test whether readout-position components causally mediate event or role query changes, and whether their removal selectively damages role selection. Not an independent confirmation, a claim of unique necessity, or evidence of the absence of a representation elsewhere.

## Inputs and frozen design
All 4 recovery seeds, 4 arms, 4 milestones. Two rich evaluation populations (unseen roles and unseen quartets), both voices. Use 128 source sentences per population/voice (512 queries), sampled without replacement by a fixed Python Random seed from frozen source ordering; same indices across checkpoints/arms and voices. This is a uniform source sample, not an independent quartet-level sample. Keep all four queries together. Verify single changed token, identical readout positions, four distinct gold candidates and candidate set. Hash sampled encodings, parent manifests and checkpoint files through inherited manifest, script and protocol. Verify full-population native scores against archived recovery results before intervention. Do not render original protected/DEV/reserved/LI fixtures. No hyperparameter or site selection from results in this phase.

## Interventions
The three blocks each provide four projected attention-head outputs, full attention output, MLP output and block residual output, at the answer readout position only (21 sites). Collect clean within-model activations. For each site replace the recipient with the matched donor from the identical source: role swap (query index XOR 1), event swap (XOR 2). Also replace with pair mean for each contrast, and zero individual projected heads. Total 96 conditions per model/population/voice. The latter is a perturbation, not a guarantee of naturalistic ablation. Head replacement is implemented as a delta added to native attention output; shared output bias stays fixed. No cross-model patching. No attention-weight intervention. Other tokens remain unchanged at the intervention site, and downstream states are recomputed normally. Record attention mass at changed query tokens descriptively; it does not establish routing or causality. The last residual swap is a positive control, not circuit discovery.

## Outcomes and interpretation
Measure full-vocabulary event mass, other-event mass, conditional role probability, donor-event conditional role probability, event log odds, role and donor-event role margins, event and role NLL, cross entropy, restricted strict and individual accuracy, full-vocabulary accuracy, and donor answer accuracy. Report raw post-intervention values and changes relative to the same sampled clean baseline. For role swap, a negative original role margin and increased role-donor accuracy with stable original event mass indicate selective role mediation. For event swap, decreased original event mass and increased other-event mass plus donor-event role preservation indicate event mediation. For pair-mean or zero ablation, compare changes in event and role NLL; disruption without donor transfer can be nonspecific. Do not divide by near-zero clean contrasts in collapsed models. Distinguish a role-token relay from correct entity binding: role swapping alone is insufficient to prove abstraction. Analyse all sites, retain negative and mixed results. Use seeds as experimental units; no treating queries or sites as independent replicates. No inferential tests on these selected seeds. Any subsequent site selection, cutoff or intervention derived here requires independent validation. A double dissociation across components is a candidate causal finding, not a required result.

## Validation and sealing
CPU toy smoke test: paired-token audit, native forward match, per-head reconstruction, no-op self patches at every site, all interventions, last residual donor identity. On actual checkpoints verify head reconstruction (atol .003, rtol .0002), native and self-patch logits (atol .0001, rtol .00001), archived native scores (inherited tolerance .001), finite logits and exact loss decomposition. Halt on failure; never bypass checks. Seal each complete checkpoint report once; aggregate only after all 16 reports exist. Keep parent files immutable. Device-specific rounding and small source samples limit precision; readout-only analysis does not test all token positions or establish a full circuit. Subsequent confirmatory study should use independent seeds, uninterrupted training and richer counterfactual constructions.
