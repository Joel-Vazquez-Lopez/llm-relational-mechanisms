# ROLE-MECHANISM-REPLICATION-v0
Prospective replication conditional on the existing synthetic grammar, not an independent dataset or architecture. Freeze before training. All 16 new seeds 27101–27116, original roles_8 only, 51,200 updates each, AdamW unchanged, no optimizer reset. Checkpoints at 6400, 12800, 25600, 51200; resume restores optimizer and CPU/MPS RNG at sealed milestones. Same original schedule generator and training exposures. No early stopping or replacing unsuccessful seeds. No claim about comparative diversity effect or restart effect (no matched restart arm). No novel vocabulary.

Discovery: 128 source indices per voice in unseen_roles_rich (training quartets, never-trained assignments), excluding previous causal samples. Evaluate every one of 12 heads separately for role and event swaps; select distinct pair maximizing sum of mean full-vocabulary target probability gains over baseline across voices; lexical tie break. Repeat independently at each checkpoint, no final-head back-selection. Test: 128 source indices per voice in unseen_quartets_rich, excluding all earlier intervention samples; donor and recipient use disjoint sets of known names, same source skeleton. Cross-voice donor also tested. Original and counterfactual full-vocab/union/restricted scores; fixed donor patches, all four query XOR conditions. Condition zero patches both heads with unchanged query donors; baseline is separate. Native joint performance on full original unseen roles and withheld quartets. Numerical native and self-patch checks; parent head reconstruction assertions. No fitting on causal test examples.

Primary descriptive replication criterion: at least two new independently trained models in each of two predefined categories at final checkpoint. First require native strict joint >=.95 in each population and voice; require full-vocab joint >=.95 for baseline and each within-source nonzero intervention on disjoint recipient stimuli in each voice. Transferable: >=.95 full-vocab recipient accuracy on cross-source and cross-voice role, event and joint swaps, each voice. Source-dependent event: >=.95 role transfer, >=.8 donor and <=.1 recipient accuracy for event and joint swaps, both donor types and voices. All other models recorded as unresolved, other or not behaviourally successful. Report counts among all 16 and conditional on success, Wilson intervals (descriptive; model seed is unit). Failure to meet criterion is inconclusive about population prevalence, not proof of absence. No inferential claim of predictive early role emergence; temporal checkpoint trajectories descriptive, selection repeated independently. Do not tune thresholds after seeing results. Replication is limited to frozen grammar and known names. Architecture robustness and repair separate studies. Training cost 819,200 model updates total; no runtime guarantee.
