# ROLE-REPRESENTATION-LOCALISATION-v0
Exploratory discovery only. No Transformer training. All four recovery seeds, four arms, four milestones. Parent and recovery manifests and diagnostic outputs are immutable. No protected/DEV populations.

Collect readout-position embeddings, attention contributions, MLP contributions, block outputs and final normalised readout. Verify instrumented logits against unmodified forward and native accuracy against archived outcomes. Attention/MLP outputs are contributions, not cumulative residuals.

Fit fixed ridge classifiers on the 240 TRAIN-derived quartets' never-trained permutations, test on the disjoint 60 withheld quartets. Both voices and surfaces as fit/test domains (16 combinations). Targets: queried event, queried role, and lexical vocabulary-ID rank of correct entity within its quartet (four classes). Rank is a proxy for candidate identification, not direct proof of role binding; it does not require predicting novel token IDs but may be difficult even when a model succeeds. The split is quartet-disjoint, not entity-disjoint. Event and role targets can reflect query lexical identity. Compare embedding baseline, shuffled within-source labels, and cross-voice/surface transfer. Measure paired query RMS differences separately; magnitude does not establish usable information. Probe fitting uses no test labels; no hyperparameter or site selection based on test performance. Ridge penalty 1, train-only feature standardisation. No inferential p-values over individual queries; retain seed and checkpoint identity.

A positive probe is decodability, not causal use; a negative linear probe is not evidence of absence. Subsequent candidate-specific interventions and position/lexical controls are required for mechanism claims. All reports are discovery data.
