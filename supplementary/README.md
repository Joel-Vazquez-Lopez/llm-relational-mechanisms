## Scientific standards

Every supplementary result should identify its study, source version, evaluation population, metric, analysis status and relevant paper claim. Preserve uncertainty, failed controls and limitations. Distinguish historical development figures from confirmed findings and final submission supplements. Do not duplicate frozen protocols or open protected evaluation splits. See the [evidence policy](../docs/EVIDENCE_POLICY.md).

Presentations, posters, talks and research updates are separate: [Research communications](../communications/README.md).
