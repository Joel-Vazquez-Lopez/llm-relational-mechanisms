# Scientific charter

## North star

Discover how pretrained Transformers construct, transform, select and deploy relationships expressed in human language. Potential targets include event addressing, participant binding, question-conditioned retrieval, syntactic transformations and competing output pathways.

This codebase does not claim a novel semantic-role circuit or universal cross-model mechanism.

## Principles

- The human research lead writes, runs and understands the official code.
- Distinguish descriptive observations, computational hypotheses, predictions and native causal mechanisms.
- Similar hidden-state paths, probing accuracy and activation patch outcomes do not prove algorithmic identity.
- Real pretrained LLMs and genuinely human-authored language are central to later validation.
- Preserve failures, counterexamples, valid negative results and frozen preregistration gates.
- Never spend paid inference, access sealed data, download restricted sources, or publish sensitive data without explicit researcher approval.
- Run targeted experimental comparisons, not repeated adaptive experiments with moving thresholds.

## Evidence ladder

1. Reproducible observation with provenance.
2. Strong rival explanations and a falsifiable separating prediction.
3. Bounded prospective experiment passing competence, format, numerical and baseline gates.
4. Independent confirmation on held-out source families and meaningfully different model families.
5. A narrow novel contribution after a full-text prior-art audit.
