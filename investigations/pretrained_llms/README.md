# Investigation B — Pretrained LLMs

**Question:** How do already-trained language models select and use linguistic relationships across surface constructions, including active/passive voice, cue wording and participant order?

This track includes **ML-001–036 and ML-048–055**, using pretrained model families such as Qwen and Llama. It is distinct from the scratch-model training programme.

## Evidence status

The pretrained experiments contain exploratory effects and several failed competence/transfer gates. Some task-conditioned activation interventions have strong local effects, but the record **does not establish** a unique general grammatical circuit, causal localization of a full relational algorithm, or cross-model generality.

Do not treat the synthetic scratch-Transformer findings as replicated in pretrained models. A bridging comparison requires its own controlled protocol, model/source provenance, lexical and position controls, and an appropriately independent test.
