# Model Linguistics — Cycle 3 Research Checkpoint

Date: 2026-10-27

## Core Working Hypothesis

Grammar may be realized through coordinated, context-dependent
transformations of relational information rather than through
a universal, fixed grammatical representation.

This is a research hypothesis, not an established mechanism.

## ML-028 — Causal Event Interchange

Llama 3B:
- Behavioral accuracy: 30/32
- Six registered layer/site combinations completed.
- L20 readout contrast: -1.792, negative in 31/32 cases.
- Earlier event-end interventions did not show compelling
  semantic interchange.

Qwen 7B:
- Behavioral accuracy: 31/32
- L20 readout contrast: -4.312, negative in 23/32 cases.
- Cross-construction interventions were highly disruptive
  even when donor event meaning was preserved.

Interpretation:
Late readout states causally influence answer preferences.
These results do not establish transferable grammatical
structure. Prepared-answer transfer, construction effects,
and contextual compatibility remain competing explanations.

## Research Status

The coordination hypothesis remains open.

Cycle 3 has not established a general grammatical
transformation law or identified a causal grammar circuit.

Next priorities:
- Disentangle prepared-answer transfer from event information.
- Introduce stronger lexical, construction, and identity controls.
- Evaluate prospective predictions on fresh stimuli.
- Replicate across models without assuming identical layer roles.

Preserve negative findings and avoid post-hoc success claims.
