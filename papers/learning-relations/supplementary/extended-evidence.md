## D. Extended methods and reproducibility

Use the linked protocols and study manifests as source of truth for generation, frozen identifiers, model/training settings, cohort selection, intervention donors, evaluation and inference. The current [methods overview](https://github.com/Joel-Vazquez-Lopez/llm-relational-mechanisms/blob/2db525a8f58dfc9fb4e86a03c27b908acc84ea68/papers/learning-relations/METHODS.md) (PR #13 candidate) maps the definitions. In particular, seed is the independent unit for paired training contrasts; question-level rows are not independent seed replicates. Conditional-role accuracy tie handling differs across studies; do not silently harmonise.

The existing summary-derived figures in [PR #13 figures](https://github.com/Joel-Vazquez-Lopez/llm-relational-mechanisms/tree/2db525a8f58dfc9fb4e86a03c27b908acc84ea68/papers/learning-relations/figures) remain primary-results figures; the underlying archived tables above are supplementary candidates. No new figure is implied.
