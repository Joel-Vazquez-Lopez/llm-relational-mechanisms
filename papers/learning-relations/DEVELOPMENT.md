# Scientific development

1. **Controlled exposure:** earlier ML-series work established repeated within-family exposure benefits but persistent joint-role errors. [Original manuscript](../../paper/manuscript.md).
2. **Construction and position:** EXP109–116, PHASE2 and position-counterbalance controls explored shortcuts and transfer. A parallel [construction-shift manuscript](https://github.com/Joel-Vazquez-Lopez/llm-relational-mechanisms/tree/a261bd847b4119dcc10b26a0f9890c22f17780b5/papers/learning-relations/construction-shift) preserves that line; do not silently replace either manuscript.
3. **Surface and anchoring:** factorial complexity and cross-evaluation exposed original-mapping anchoring; the separate [anchor audit](https://github.com/Joel-Vazquez-Lopez/llm-relational-mechanisms/blob/10ab4c7bf2b56d3e8c8e396eee22e2e732a41910/paper/ROLE_ANCHOR_PROBE_V0_RESULT_AUDIT.md) motivated role-diversity training.
4. **Diversity and recovery:** the prospective diversity hypothesis failed at fixed budget. Selected continuation revealed delayed learning and persistent event-only collapse.
5. **Mechanistic variation:** probes, causal swaps, pathway and binding studies identified different organisations in selected successful seeds. Independent behavioural heterogeneity recurred; mechanistic classification failed.
6. **Repair:** promising single-model recovery did not establish reliable acceleration in follow-up. Shift priority from late repair to learning origins.
7. **Origins and future:** crossed initialisation/stream design is ongoing. Independent prediction, prevention and new grammars/architectures are future studies, not completed evidence.

See [Results](RESULTS.md) for quantitative outcomes and [Experiments](EXPERIMENTS.md) for exact frozen sources.
